## Dataset

he Telco Customer Churn dataset contains customer-level information from a telecommunications company, including demographics, account details, subscribed services, contract types, payment methods, tenure, and monthly and total charges. The target variable, Churn, indicates whether a customer left the company (Yes) or remained (No). The goal of this analysis is to use logistic regression to predict the probability of customer churn and identify the factors most strongly associated with a customer's likelihood of leaving.
